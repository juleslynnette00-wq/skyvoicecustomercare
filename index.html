Frequently Asked Questions

Is SkyVoice free to use?

Yes. Filing a complaint through SkyVoice is completely free for passengers. We never charge fees or commissions.

Which airlines can I complain about?

Our directory includes over 150 airlines across Africa, the Americas, Asia-Pacific, Europe and the Middle East, from major international carriers to regional and low-cost airlines.

What information should I include?

Include your booking reference, flight date and number, a clear description of the issue, and any supporting details such as receipts, photos or correspondence with the airline.

How long does a response take?

Response times vary by airline. Many carriers respond within 14–30 days under applicable consumer-protection regulations such as EU 261/2004 or US DOT rules.

Is my personal data safe?

Your details are transmitted over an encrypted connection and shared only with the airline concerned for the purpose of resolving your complaint.
